# E34 Lore and E34 Chassis

Reviewed 5 November 2026. This review informed changes to the existing application. It does not authorize copying another site's text, photographs or forum content.

## What is useful

E34 Chassis's strongest feature is concrete, photograph-led walkthroughs. Its [brake guide](https://www.e34chassis.com/brake-pads/) places tools, parts and difficulty before the operations, with photographs around the work. Its [door-panel guide](https://www.e34chassis.com/door-panel-removal/) makes hidden fixings, clips and connections easier to follow. These are useful editorial patterns for original E34 Lore guides.

The [home navigation](https://www.e34chassis.com/) groups maintenance, upgrades, troubleshooting and general information. Several prominent Guides/video/overheating destinations inspected resolved to the home page rather than a dedicated index. Its [Downloads page](https://www.e34chassis.com/downloads/) provides a resource shelf, including wiring documents by year. E34 Lore should have working task indexes and direct links to specific destinations.

The [coolant guide](https://www.e34chassis.com/coolant/) covers several configurations and includes a separately credited owner anecdote. E34 Lore can make applicability easier to assess by keeping engine, production, market, source revision and unresolved steps beside each guide. That is more useful than expanding the apparent guide count without expanding verified coverage.

The [public forum](https://www.e34chassis.com/community/) displayed unrelated advertisements during this review, with most named technical categories showing no topics. This is a dated observation, not an estimate of genuine membership. E34 Lore's moderation, verified-email contributions and reasoned feedback need to work before inviting members; promotional membership totals are unnecessary.

## Provenance

The E34 Chassis home page acknowledges references to E34BMW.net, updates to some material, and documentation of its own. Individual pages carry some contributor/source credits. Those statements do not establish the authorship or permission status of every item. Visual style also does not establish whether a site was AI-generated. E34 Lore should publish its own source and image credits instead of speculating about that site's production process.

## E34 Lore's position

Both sites focus on the E34. The opportunity is to cover an owner's complete task within that focus:

| Owner task | E34 Lore implementation |
|---|---|
| Find the relevant car/configuration | Model and installed-engine context, source scope, current/previous ownership |
| Identify a component | Owned factual part-number index, source drawing positions, interactive figures and separate 3D references |
| Understand the work | Original source-based walkthroughs, tools, checkpoints, visual references, persistent checklists and exact page citations |
| Resolve an uncertainty | Linked technical discussion, structured question templates, Helpful / Bad advice with a required explanation, technical-review proposals |
| Preserve the result and the story | Private service history plus explicitly published garage stories; current and previously owned cars |
| Find community knowledge again | Global reference/garage/discussion search, controlled tags, related discussions on canonical pages and editorial starting points |

The 48 editorial resources are prompts and indexes. They are not 48 completed repair procedures. The six original manual-based guides retain their identification, inspection or planning scope and draft technical-review status. The compiled part-number count does not mean every interchange or ordering condition is verified.

## Changes made in this milestone

- E34 Lore branding and `e34lore.com` / `www.e34lore.com` domain bindings; existing project and data retained.
- Working Walkthroughs, Parts, Workshop, Community, Garages and Shop destinations; technical encyclopedia remains available from the home and footer.
- Step navigation and source figures on applicable walkthroughs, plus three real, credited engine-family reference photographs. The photographs do not certify an installed engine variant.
- Current/previous ownership records and optional public garage stories, separated from private maintenance history.
- A clearly labeled coming-soon merchandise shop and member parts-exchange preview. No invented products, prices, stock, checkout or trades.
- See the [community architecture](community-architecture.md) for discussion and moderation.

## Next original content investment

Create one complete photographed exemplar for one verified configuration, such as door-card access, using owner-created or properly licensed photographs and a source-checked procedure. Record fasteners, cable routing, clip condition, reassembly checks and technical review. Extend coverage after validating differences. The existing M50 pump planning guide must not be relabeled complete while its source dependencies remain unresolved.

Use real member questions and search failures to prioritize subsequent procedures. A smaller set of accurate, illustrated walkthroughs connected to the car and parts record is more useful than a large unreviewed collection.
