# Interactive Workshop

The E34 and E30 Workshops share a Three.js viewer with separate vehicle references and catalog contexts. Open a panel, select a component, explore its catalog drawing, or choose a guided repair.

The standalone demo includes an original model and sample coolant-pump drawing for each series. Select **Sample coolant pump** to follow the component into the demo catalog.

## Explore

The viewer supports rotation, zoom, pan, camera presets, component search, isolation, body visibility and an expanded view. Supported doors, hood, trunk, glove box, sunroof and fuel flap have opening controls. Selecting an opening panel also retains its part information.

Exploded views separate modeled assemblies and their components. Restoring the assembled view returns them to their installed positions. Wiring views provide harness isolation, sleeve separation and links to the matching electrical references.

## Repair

The 1991 E30 325i Coupe and 1995 E34 525i automatic sedan references include guided transmission, brake, suspension, cooling, driveshaft, differential, wheel-bearing, hub and air-filter jobs.

Start a job from Repair or its matching catalog position. The walkthrough shows access steps, removal and refit operations, tools and manual references. Available jobs depend on the selected reference and modeled parts. Technical specifications retain their source and hardware restrictions.

## Connected part references

A selected component can carry its part number, catalog position, reference model and revision between the scanner, catalog, written guide and 3D repair. Signed-in members can save an identification to a car and reopen it in My Garage.

Public share links keep the public reference details. Private account, vehicle and scan identifiers are excluded from sharing.

## Views and revisions

Model files are immutable and checked against their manifest hashes. Saved views and build projects resolve their recorded revision. Rendering runs when the view changes, and the viewer releases its graphics resources when closed.

Model fit and movement illustrate the repair. Confirm the installed hardware and applicable manual before using a procedure on a car.
