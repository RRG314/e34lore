# Advertising operations

Initial state: display advertising off, both placements off, no approved retailers, no published offers, no sponsorships and no fabricated revenue. Google ownership verification and review submission are complete; approval is pending. No AdSense serving script, Auto ads or payment processor is deployed.

The owner has two possible display placements, header and footer, with at most one selected campaign per placement. Campaigns need an actual approved sponsor, creative permission, a validated destination, campaign dates and an explicit state. Overlapping campaigns use recorded priority and ID ordering; they never increase the two-ad limit. Ads are labeled with the advertiser. Text and licensed images are supported; arbitrary HTML, scripts, autoplay audio, misleading downloads and advertising popups are not.

Eligible sections are the public homepage, parts reference, articles, encyclopedia and community index/editorial pages. Entire repair/walkthrough, diagnostic, workshop, private garage, public garage/journal, account, staff and shop areas are excluded. Server and client repeat the route restrictions. Public requests cannot enable owner preview or read drafts/contacts/contracts. Preview is an administrator/MFA operation and does not record public impressions.

Before enabling a direct campaign, verify the written agreement, creative rights, sponsor destination and dates. Preview the saved creative, then explicitly enable the approved campaign, its placement and the global display setting. Use a reason for each change. Disable the global setting or campaign to withdraw future renders. Already downloaded images cannot be recalled.

AdSense is optional. A successful application does not configure ad units or satisfy regional consent obligations. Actual approval, appropriate ad-unit identifiers, the current Google policies, a required certified CMP where applicable and owner/privacy approval are external prerequisites for a future serving-code activation. This release intentionally has no path that silently turns ownership tags into ads. [Google site review](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/12169212) and [Google consent requirements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554116).

Network tracking links require a current agreement and authorized exact hosts/query parameters. Use the generator only with actual public provider identifiers. Never paste credentials into a URL. An owner-requested HEAD check follows at most four authorized redirects, has bounded deadlines, forwards no member credentials, and records ok/broken/blocked. Confirm that automated checks are permitted by the program. A blocked request is not proof that a product is unavailable. Re-check before the 30-day checked-link window expires.

Feed synchronization is not falsely advertised as active: no approved feed or credential was supplied. Local CSV/JSON/XML normalization and reviewed batch import are implemented. Scheduled remote synchronization remains disabled until an authorized provider connector is configured. No prohibited scraping or paid subscription was introduced.
