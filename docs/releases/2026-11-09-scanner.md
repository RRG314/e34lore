# E34 Lore release 47 — photo finder, 9 November 2026

Published to https://e34lore.com/scanner as version 47 after the owner explicitly authorized the tested photo workflow, including gallery uploads, and final production checks passed. This supersedes the local-only hold for this bounded feature. It does not release exact-part experiments, the animated M50 repair workbench, installed-configuration tools, sound diagnosis or the private authoring studio.

## Included

- Public /scanner with camera or gallery input, up to three automatically retained photos, local comparison and explicit catalog selection. No box, number or account is required to compare a photo.
- Account-scoped device drafts, reload recovery and explicit guest-to-member handoff. Drafts expire after 24 hours of inactivity when next opened. Photos are not uploaded to a recognition service or garage storage.
- Full OEM assembly drawing and source-position table, canonical part/fitment information, existing selected 3D assembly location and linked service material on the same page.
- A shared written ten-step center-bearing reference from the supplied manual, including removal dependencies, tools, press/puller use, shaft marks, preload and hardware-specific tightening values. The bearing inspection route is released; other interactive repair routes remain 404.
- Optional private garage result/candidate and notes through existing ownership rules. A reload no longer relabels a catalog-only choice as a read marking.
- Public navigation, roadmap and privacy wording match the released scope. Exact-part experiment controls are excluded from production and their worker actions reject production calls.

## Limits and rollback

This is assisted component-category comparison, not exact OEM identification. The small convenience evaluation remains 3/9 coarse top-one positives. Results require physical comparison and explicit catalog choice. The bearing highlight is a source-backed assembly location, not measured part CAD. Latest physical Android and broad confusable-part acceptance are not certified by the desktop/mobile-viewport tests. Missing model links and service procedures remain on the roadmap.

No new database migration, provider setting, security grant, commercial activation or geometry asset change is included. Automatic publication on Git push remains off. Roll back application files by deploying saved Site version 46. Account and garage data do not require restoration for this application-only release.
