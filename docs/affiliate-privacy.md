# Commercial disclosure and privacy review

The public policy is `/partnerships`, linked from the footer and offer disclosures. Each actual offer includes: “E34 Lore may earn a commission when you purchase through certain retailer links. This does not affect our technical compatibility information.” It appears with the offer rather than only on a remote policy page. Conditional fitment notes and independent retailer responsibility remain explicit. Sponsored creatives are labeled Advertisement and identify the sponsor.

[FTC disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) calls for material relationships to be understandable and placed with the endorsement. This implementation supports clear disclosure; it is not a certification of legal compliance. Operator contact/legal identity, final contracts, regional privacy obligations and the Google consent configuration still require owner/legal review before additional advertising services are activated.

Private member emails, account IDs, full VINs, garage records, diagnostic responses and browsing history are not included in outbound links or commercial counters. Retailer/network cookies and processing begin under their own terms when a visitor follows an external link. The redirect returns no-referrer and no-store headers. No private advertiser or financial record is returned to public visitors.

Commercial counting has a separate off-by-default consent switch; the older page-count consent is not repurposed. Opted-in clicks/impressions aggregate by date and saved link/campaign. The counter tables have no visitor, account, IP, search or referrer fields. Retention is 90 days, separate from financial evidence and provider logs. Counters are not unique-visitor statistics, conversion proof or revenue. Public counter endpoints can include automation, which is disclosed.

Remote product/creative image requests are withheld without the commercial opt-in, except same-origin authorized images. There is no third-party advertising script in this release. Actual API secrets are never public fields; references point to a separately managed server secret store. Business contact, agreements, provider reports and revenue remain administrator/MFA restricted.

Do not sell member data, populate private fields with guessed payee information, or turn pending program applications into claimed partnerships. Google payee details were entered by the owner in Google's interface and were not collected into this repository.
