## Research retained

- Browser recording lifecycle: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MediaRecorder — duration/size bounds, late permission cleanup, codec compatibility and per-device testing are required before capture acceptance.
- YAMNet reference implementation: https://github.com/tensorflow/models/tree/master/research/audioset/yamnet — a general sound-event embedding/classification candidate, not an E34 fault classifier. Model/code rights do not grant rights to training or third-party recordings.
- Real generic engine recording and attribution example: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:5_cylinder_engine_sound.ogg — Volvo850T5, Jonas Tittmann, CC BY-SA4.0. Not an E34 recording or known-fault ground truth. Not imported.
- CC0 sound-effect source candidate: https://opengameart.org/content/car-engine-start-01 — generic game sound effects are not mechanical diagnosis evidence. Not imported or used as a test corpus.
- Independent shop discovery source: https://members.bimrs.org/find-a-shop.html — link/reference only, not bulk-copied or used to invent E34 endorsements. Business identity/expertise must be checked against first-party evidence and dated community reports.
- Geocoding policy: https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/ — service limits/identification/caching rules need to be respected; no service integration or precise-location transmission was enabled.

The next servicing content dependency after owner UI acceptance is source-specific inspection/access indexing for another existing guide, followed by wiring/diagnostic evidence. Photo and audio need separate real-media evaluation gates while sharing component context. Do not release them on the strength of the navigation or arithmetic tests.
