# E34 Lore community policy review

Draft for owner and legal review. The current [published community policies](https://e34lore.com/community/policies) continue to govern the site.

## Community and technical evidence

Members may share E34 projects, questions and experience. Describe the actual car, the source used and what was observed. Mark estimates, unknown fitment and work in progress. Upload or link material only when you have the right to share it. Remove identifying vehicle documents, precise home locations and other personal information before publishing.

Helpful and Bad advice feedback apply to the version the reader saw. Explain Bad advice with the factual problem or missing context. Use the report flow for harassment, private information, dangerous instructions or policy violations. A member’s accepted answer or a popular comment represents community feedback. A separate technical review checks the claim and its source.

## Guest posts and moderation

Guest questions and replies are held for review. Moderators should check for spam, personal information, citations and a clear question before making content public. Document a reason for any decision that affects another member. Use the least restrictive appropriate action. A member may appeal an eligible restriction from the community settings area. Assign the appeal to another qualified person when available. Set response expectations only once staffing supports them.

## Privacy and deletion

A garage vehicle, journal entry or photo is private until the member explicitly chooses to share it through the applicable publication flow. Published snapshots exclude private source notes, amounts, mileage and private vehicle identifiers. Review the fields and photo before publishing. A person who already downloaded a public photo may retain their copy.

A verified account holder can request a data export and use the account deletion flow. The deletion interface explains which owned discussions, replies and garage records are removed. Community staff permissions do not expose private garage and journal source records. Optional analytics stores aggregate route opens by day. Decide and publish how the site handles separately operated hosting, email, platform and encrypted-backup records.

## Owner decisions before adoption

Approve or amend the proposed retention periods, the private contact channel for abuse/privacy requests, the escalation and appeal coverage, and the applicable publication rights. Review account deletion, backup expiration and any legal holds together. Confirm that the policy matches the live retention jobs and permission model. The owner publishes approved language through a separately reviewed release.
