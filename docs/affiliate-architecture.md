# Affiliate and advertising architecture

This extends the retained E34 Lore Site, dedicated Supabase project, native community and private owner dashboard. It does not create another backend, retailer inventory store or payment processor.

The twenty new business tables live in **e34_private** with RLS and no direct client table grants. They cover networks, programs, applications, retailers, credential references, import jobs, products, identifiers, match reviews, offers, links, click aggregates, commissions, sponsorships, payment receipts, placements, campaigns, campaign aggregates, settings and the revenue ledger. Financial, contractual and contact data are never returned by public offer/ad functions. Actual secrets are not table fields: credential metadata accepts only `vault:name` or `env:NAME` references.

Privileged public RPC facades are security invokers. Their fixed-search-path private implementations require the existing **administrator role plus AAL2**. Moderator status alone is insufficient. Saves are table/column-whitelisted, typed by PostgreSQL, optimistic-version checked, reasoned and audited. They cannot modify staff roles, BMW catalog data or arbitrary tables. Ledger entries and sponsor receipts are append-only through this API; reversals lock their original earning and cannot exceed its remaining amount.

`/manage/partners` is part of the existing management area and uses the existing account/session boundaries. It provides Overview, Applications, Retailers, Product matching, Advertising and Revenue sections. The unmatched OEM queue uses a 25-row keyset cursor and never modifies the catalog. Activity reports expose optional 30-day retailer referrals, linked-part identities and campaign counts; these are not conversions. Monthly ledger totals are separated by income channel and currency. Other records are bounded to 100 recent search matches; private exports require the signed-in owner. Source research and common application copy are separate from private live status, contracts and provider records.

Public catalog records call `public_affiliate_offers` with their canonical part and, where selected, exact source position. Only current approved programs, active retailers, reviewed matches, published offers and checked links can appear. Price, availability and shipping observations have independent authorization and validity dates. A product-identity change invalidates its review and withdraws publication. Direct supersessions need the directed source edge and the replacement's actual identifier.

`/go/[id]` resolves an opaque saved ID through the current server gate. It does not accept an arbitrary destination, forward account credentials or cache redirects. Tracking and product hosts use exact allowlists. Query parameters need program authorization. Owner-requested link checks use bounded HEAD requests, validate each redirect hop, forward no credentials, and distinguish blocked checks from broken links. A check does not activate a program or obtain retailer approval.

Display campaigns use only the two registered placements, server-approved public section families, dated campaigns and approved sponsors. Public rendering repeats the route boundary and maximum-two rule. Private pages, repair procedures, diagnostics and the workshop do not mount ads. Advertising starts disabled. Verification tags and ads.txt do not load AdSense code. Actual AdSense ad units, approval and applicable certified-consent configuration remain separate external dependencies.

Authorized CSV/JSON/XML files are normalized by `tools/affiliate_feeds.py`. The owner previews the normalized JSON and imports it through an MFA-gated transaction. SKU/hash deduplication, error receipts and pending identifier matches preserve canonical BMW data. No retailer scraping is implemented. Scheduled synchronization remains disabled until an authorized provider connector, credentials and feed agreement exist; the database rejects pretending a schedule is active without that setup.

The independent commercial-count opt-in defaults off. Counters store only date and link/campaign, retain 90 days through a dedicated cron job, and are separate from network conversions and the financial ledger. No member identity or private vehicle context is appended to outbound links.

## Launch readiness and general retailer links

`affiliate_launch_readiness` is an administrator/AAL2-only invoker facade over a fixed-search-path private projection. Per-retailer full-data counts and up to three scoped catalog samples reuse `public_affiliate_offers`; saved publication flags are not counted as proof of public availability. It returns no credentials or payout information. `public_affiliate_retailers` exposes only saved link IDs and retailer names for current approved product-free links that pass the existing resolver. The public disclosure page renders them with nearby disclosure, sponsored/nofollow attributes and the same default-off counting choice. A general tool-store referral has no OEM compatibility assertion. Both projections are additive; canonical catalog data and existing commercial editing/approval APIs are unchanged.
